Alpine Lakes & Tarns

By Ana Ruiz · Updated 3 May 2026

Alpine lakes form where glaciers carved deep basins. They’re cold, clear and often surrounded by steep cirques. This guide covers how to find them, when to go, and what to pack.

Best months to visit

Most high lakes thaw in late June. September brings fewer people and the larches turn gold — a favourite of photographers.

  • June: ice on the shore, snow on the trail
  • July – August: wildflowers, warm afternoons
    • Watch for afternoon thunderstorms
  • September: larch season
